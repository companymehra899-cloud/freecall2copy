SpeakFree Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

SpeakFree is an English speaking practice app that lets users make anonymous live voice calls with other learners.

1. Age requirement

SpeakFree is for users 18 years of age or older. Random voice chat with strangers is not intended for children. By using the app you confirm that you are at least 18.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details you provide (name, email) if you sign up
  • Microphone audio during a live call (streamed peer-to-peer, not stored on our servers)
  • Temporary matchmaking data in Firebase Firestore while connecting a call
  • Google Play purchase receipts if you buy the VIP pass
  • Device identifiers needed for Firebase and Google Play Billing

3. Microphone and voice calls

Microphone permission is required for live voice practice. Audio is sent directly between devices using WebRTC. We do not record, store, or sell call audio.

4. Random stranger voice chat

Find Partner matches you with another learner at random. Do not share personal information (address, phone, banking details, photos of minors). You can Report or Block a partner during a call. Abuse, harassment, sexual content involving minors, or illegal activity is not allowed and may result in account action.

5. User-generated content (UGC) safety

Voice and in-app chat are user-generated. Users can:

  • Report a partner for harassment, hate, spam, or illegal content
  • Block a partner and end the call immediately

We review reports and may restrict accounts that violate these rules.

6. How data is used

We use data only to run matchmaking, authentication, purchases, and safety features. Temporary signaling documents are deleted after a call connects.

7. Sharing

We share data with Google Firebase (auth, Firestore) and Google Play Billing as needed to operate the app. We do not sell personal data.

8. Contact

For privacy or safety requests, contact the developer through the SpeakFree Google Play listing.
